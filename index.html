03 Oversight

Decision rights, always human

AI prepares the room.
People make the call.

Automation should make judgment more accountable, not less visible. Jury is built around explicit human decision rights at every consequential step.

Calibrate before scoring

Use shared anchor submissions to expose different interpretations of your rubric before independent review begins.

Interrogate disagreement

See the exact criterion, evidence and rationale behind a gap. Record the resolution without erasing the original scores.

Own the final record

Judges can override any recommendation, add context and sign the final decision with a plain-language explanation.

A necessary caveat: no scoring system can guarantee fairness. Jury makes criteria, evidence and interventions easier to inspect; organisers remain responsible for testing outcomes and listening to participants.
